#ODD-EVEN GAME:-
A simple version of cricket that is played between two-player, where players are allowed to pick numbers between 1 and 6 to score runs while batting.
If the number picked by batter will be same as the number picked by bowler then it will be a out.
The player with higher batting score will win the match.
The code is written in Python language.

#Relating to Cricket:-
Like a real world cricket, the game has a toss at the first, player who won the toss can decide to bat-first or bowl-first
there is fixed over of 6 balls.
Winner will be decided by total runs made by the players.
Illegal input(numbers above 6) are treated as fouls.

#Rules:-
1. Toss: one have to choose even and automatically other will get odd. Both have to pick a number between 1 and 6, and the winner will be decide by the sum of two numbers picked is eve or odd
   * if the sum is even, the player who choose Even will win the toss.
   * if the sum is odd, the player who choose Odd will win the toss.
2. Vaildation number: Only numbers between 1 and 6 are allowed, any number greater will consider as a foul and the batter will lose his batting as a penalty.
3. Match length: The match will be of 1 over(includes 6 balls)
4. Inning: Both players will get to chance to do batting and bowling, one after the other.
5. Scoring: The sum of the numbers picked by both the players while the time of batting will be there final score.
6. Result: The player with the higher score will win the game.

#Feature:-
* Toss based Even or Odd
* Validation with foul detection
* Ball by ball score tracking using list
* Automatic calculation
* Winner declaration
